# Recharge & Consumption Records

Track purchases, balance, and order history.

- [Transactions](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-transactions.html) — payment and recharge records
- [My Proxies](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-my-orders.html) — active and expired proxy orders
- [Purchase](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-purchase.html) — buy new packages or top up balance

Rotating usage is deducted from traffic quota; long-term/custom by validity period.
