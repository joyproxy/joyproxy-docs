# Top Up Your JoyProxy Balance with USDT (TRC20): A Step-by-Step Guide

JoyProxy accepts **TRON USDT (TRC20)** as a top-up method for your account balance. It is fast, works from any TRON wallet or exchange, and JoyProxy charges **no deposit fee**. This guide walks through the exact flow—getting a deposit address, sending the transfer, and claiming the credit.

## The rules in one paragraph

**1 USDT = $1.** The minimum deposit is **1 USDT** , and you pick whole-dollar amounts in the Payment Hub. Any extra cents you send are still credited 1:1. Crypto top-ups earn the **same bonus tiers** as every other payment channel. Only **official USDT on the TRON network** is accepted—see the safety notes below.

## Before you start

  * A TRON wallet holding USDT-TRC20. **TronLink** is recommended, but any TRON wallet or an exchange withdrawal to the TRON network works.
  * A small amount of **TRX** in your wallet to cover the network fee (you pay the TRON network, not JoyProxy).
  * You must be signed in to your JoyProxy account.



## Step by step

  1. **Open the Payment Hub** and choose **USDT (TRC20)**. The first time you select it, JoyProxy generates a deposit address that is **uniquely and permanently yours**.
  2. **Copy the address or scan the QR code.** Send official TRC20 USDT to it from TronLink, another TRON wallet, or an exchange withdrawal set to the **TRON (TRC20)** network.
  3. **Keep the Hub open.** After about 8 seconds you can click **“I’ve paid.”** JoyProxy then watches the chain for roughly **5 minutes** and credits your balance automatically once the transfer confirms.
  4. **If you closed the window,** go to **My Account** and claim the deposit there. (The claim card only appears once you already have a USDT deposit address.)



## Send only official TRON USDT

Your deposit address accepts **official Tether USDT on the TRON (TRC20) network only**. Reputable wallets and exchanges label this token as “USDT” with the network shown as **TRON / TRC20** —select that network when you withdraw.

**Do not** send USDT on other chains (ERC20 on Ethereum, BEP20 on BNB Chain, etc.) or any non-official/“fake” token. Funds sent on the wrong network or via an unsupported token will **not** be credited and are generally unrecoverable.

## Fees, timing, and amounts

  * **Deposit fee:** none from JoyProxy. You only pay the standard TRON network fee (a small amount of TRX) from your own wallet.
  * **Timing:** most transfers confirm on-chain within a couple of minutes; the Hub polls for about 5 minutes.
  * **Amounts:** minimum 1 USDT; whole-dollar values are selectable, and surplus cents are credited at 1:1.



## Troubleshooting

  * **Balance not updated?** Confirm the transfer shows as successful on a TRON explorer, then open **My Account** and use the claim card.
  * **Sent on the wrong network?** ERC20/BEP20 transfers never reach the TRON address and cannot be auto-credited—always double-check the network before withdrawing from an exchange.
  * **Using an old address?** If your USDT address mapping was reset or reissued, only your **current** address counts. Deposits to a previous address are not credited automatically.
