# AI Assistant

Chat with JoyProxy AI for product, billing, and setup questions.

- Visit [AI Assistant](https://www.joyproxy.com/products/ai-customer-service.html)
- Click **Open Live Chat** on the page (or use the floating chat on any marketing page)
- Ask about plans, endpoint generation, authorization, or troubleshooting
- Request human support escalation when needed via [Support](https://www.joyproxy.com/service.html)
