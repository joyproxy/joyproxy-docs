# Purchase Rotating Residential

Traffic-based residential packages for high-frequency rotation. On the purchase page, select **Residential** and the **Rotating** mode.

- Sign in and open [Purchase → Rotating](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-purchase.html?tab=short-term)
- Confirm network type is **Residential**
- Choose a traffic package and validity period
- Pay with PayPal or account balance
- After payment, view active orders in [My Proxies](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-my-orders.html)

Rotating orders stay active while traffic remains and validity has not expired.
