# Choose a network and buy traffic

Rotating is billed **per GB**. The pack stays usable until the traffic is consumed (and any validity shown at checkout still applies). Larger packs usually lower the unit price.

**Residential, Mobile, and Business / ISP each have their own price.** Compare packs on [Pricing](https://www.joyproxy.com/pricing.html) before you pay.

## Pick a network

| Network | Choose it when | Product |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Residential** | Home-ISP style exits and the widest geo menu | [Residential](https://www.joyproxy.com/products/proxy-residential.html) |
| **Mobile** | Carrier (4G/5G) exits for apps or mobile ads | [Mobile](https://www.joyproxy.com/products/proxy-mobile.html) |
| **Business / ISP** | Targets expect an ISP / business ASN | [Business / ISP](https://www.joyproxy.com/products/proxy-business.html) |

You can hold more than one network on the same account. Each order appears separately in [My Proxies](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-my-orders.html).

## Buy in the console

1. Sign in and open [Purchase → Rotating](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-purchase.html?tab=short-term).
2. Set **network type** to Residential, Mobile, or Business / ISP.
3. Select a **traffic package**. Match size to a real workload (a few GB for trials; more for production crawls).
4. Pay in checkout: **card**, **Apple Pay**, **Google Pay**, **PayPal**, **WeChat Pay**, **UPI**, **USDT (TRC20)**, or **account balance**.
5. Wait until the order shows as active under [My Proxies](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-my-orders.html).

> **Tip**
>
> Apply the **$5 sign-up credit** on the first eligible pack if the checkout offers it.

## After payment

- **Usage** on the network console shows remaining GB.
- Endpoints keep working as long as traffic remains. You do not buy a new pack every time you generate a username.
- When GB runs low, return to [Purchase → Rotating](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-purchase.html?tab=short-term) and add another pack on the same network.
