# Custom proxy (Residential, Business / ISP, Datacenter)

Custom proxies let you **assign region** and **set rotation interval** before generating endpoints. The result is still a stable host:port per line, with rotation behavior you control.

## Typical workflow

1. **Purchase** a custom-capable package.
2. **Assign region** — Pick country/region (and city where available).
3. **Set rotation interval** — Define how often the exit may change, per product rules.
4. **Users & Whitelist** and **Generate endpoints** — Same as static.
5. **Connect** in code, OS, browser, or third-party apps.

Available on **Residential**, **Business / ISP**, and **Datacenter** where custom SKU is sold.
