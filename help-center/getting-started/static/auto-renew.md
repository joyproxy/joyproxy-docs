# Auto-renew

Static lines are billed **per IP for a duration**. Auto-renew charges **account balance** before expiry so the same `host:port` stays yours. You can turn this off anytime.

Keep enough balance for the next period. Compare plans on <a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/pricing.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Pricing</a>.

## Turn it on

1. Open <a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-my-orders.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">My Proxies</a> → Static.
2. Open **Auto-renew** on the order.
3. Choose a **Renewal period** (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Half-year, Annual — as shown in the modal).
4. Confirm the estimated amount and next charge date, then **Enable auto-renew**.

If remaining time is already inside the lead window, the modal offers **Enable and charge now** — that extends validity immediately.

## Charge timing

The modal shows **Next charge** and **Current expiry**. Lead time follows the plan, for example:

| Plan family | Charged |
| --- | --- |
| Daily | 12 hours before expiry |
| Weekly | 2 days before expiry |
| Longer plans | 7 days before expiry |

After a successful charge, validity extends. Your **host:port** and purchased region stay the same.

Paying a manual renew while auto-renew is on extends immediately; the next automatic charge follows the new expiry.

## Turn it off

Use **Turn off auto-renew**. The order stays active until the current expiry.
