# Buy a Static line

Static is priced **per IP** for a duration (day, week, month, longer plans as shown at checkout). There is no GB cap on the line itself—you pay for the allocation and the period.

**Residential, Business / ISP, and Datacenter each have their own Static price.** Compare on [Pricing](https://www.joyproxy.com/pricing.html).

## Steps

1. Open [Purchase → Static](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-purchase.html?tab=long-term).
2. Select **Residential**, **Business / ISP**, or **Datacenter**.
3. Search or pick **country** (province / city when the page offers it). This geo is **locked for the order**.
4. Choose duration and quantity.
5. Pay in checkout: card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, WeChat Pay, UPI, USDT (TRC20), or account balance.
6. Open [My Proxies](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-my-orders.html) and wait until the allocation is active.

Need several cities under one order? [Custom](../custom/purchase.md) assigns region **per port** after purchase.

## After checkout

- Enable [Auto-renew](auto-renew.md) so the line extends from account balance, or renew from My Proxies / Purchase.
- Generate `host:port` next: [Authorization](authorization.md) then [Generate endpoints](generate-endpoints.md).
