# Register

Create a JoyProxy account to purchase products and manage proxies in the console.

1. Open [Sign up](https://www.joyproxy.com/register.html).
2. Enter your email and password and complete the form.
3. Complete **email activation** if prompted ([Email activation](email-activation.md)).
4. [Log in](login.md) and open the [dashboard overview](overview.md).
