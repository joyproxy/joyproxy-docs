# Business Proxies console

Business Proxies use office and commercial broadband exits. They are billed by traffic (GB) and rotate through a shared gateway. They fit B2B portals, business accounts, and collection jobs that need a commercial ASN with frequent IP changes.

In the left sidebar **Proxies** group, click **Business Proxies**:

<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-proxy-business.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Open the Business Proxies console</a> · <a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/products/proxy-business.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Business Proxies product page</a>

For a fixed `host:port` ISP line, use the <a href="isp-proxies.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ISP Proxies console</a>.

---

## Rotating-only product

The **Business Proxies** console sells Rotating Proxies only (GB packs). There is no Static or Custom SKU here:

- Billing: buy a prepaid traffic pack; GB is deducted from actual proxy traffic until the pack is empty;
- Access: one gateway over the business broadband pool. Target country and city. Rotate per request or keep a sticky session.

The flow matches the <a href="../getting-started/rotating/README.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Rotating Proxies guide</a>.

## Common tasks

### 1. Buy Business Proxies traffic
Open the **Buy** tab and pick a pack. Network details: <a href="../getting-started/rotating/network-types.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Network types</a>.

### 2. Set authorization
Open **Users & Whitelist** and complete authorization. The Business Proxies gateway uses User/Pass. Full rules: <a href="../getting-started/rotating/authentication.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Users & Whitelist</a>.

### 3. Generate endpoints
On **Endpoint generator**, pick the target geo. See <a href="../getting-started/rotating/extract-ip.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Generate endpoints</a>.

### 4. Watch remaining traffic
On **Usage**, check used and remaining GB. For always-on jobs, enable <a href="../getting-started/rotating/auto-buy-traffic.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Auto-buy traffic</a> on **My Proxies**.
