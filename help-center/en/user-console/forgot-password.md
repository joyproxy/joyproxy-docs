# Forgot password

If you forgot the Log In password, you can reset it from the registered email in a few minutes. You do not need to wait for a support review.

<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/forgot-password.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Open the reset password page</a>

You can also open the <a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/login.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Log In page</a> and click **Forgot password?** under the password field.

---

## Self-service steps

1. On the reset page, enter the full email address bound to your JoyProxy account.
2. Click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the inbox for a password-reset message (if nothing arrives in about 2 minutes, check spam).
4. Open the unique reset link and go to the new-password page.
5. Enter a new password (at least 6 characters; mix of letters and numbers is recommended) and submit.
6. After the reset succeeds, return to Log In with the new password.

---

## Security notes

- **Link lifetime**: Reset links expire (usually after 30 minutes) and work only once. If a link is dead, submit the form again.
- **Not the in-console change path**: If you are already signed in and you still know the current password, change it in the console instead: **My Account** → **Profile** → **Change Password**. You do not need the email reset flow.
- **Official staff will not ask for your password**: JoyProxy engineers and Live Chat agents never ask you to send a Log In password. If someone in chat or social channels asks for it “for debugging,” refuse.
