# Email activation

Email Sign Up requires a one-time activation. It blocks bulk bot registrations and makes sure recharge invoices, expiry reminders, and other notices can reach you.

---

## Activation flow

1. **Check the activation email**: After you submit the Sign Up form, JoyProxy sends an activation message to the address you used.
2. **Open the verification link**: In the email, click the blue activation button or the full URL. A new tab opens the activation page:
   - On success, the page shows **Account activated!** and a button to continue to Log In;
   - If you see **The activation link is invalid or has expired.**, the link was already used or timed out. Request a new one.
3. **Log In**: Return to the <a href="login.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Log In page</a> and sign in with your email and password.

---

## If the email does not arrive

If nothing arrives within about 3 minutes:

1. **Check spam and promotions**: Some providers (Outlook, QQ Mail, and many company mail servers) file system mail under Junk, Subscriptions, or Promotions.
2. **Resend the link**: Open the <a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/login.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Log In page</a>, enter your email and password, and try to sign in. The page shows that the account is not activated. Click **Resend activation email** to get a new link.
3. **Check the address**: Confirm there is no typo (for example `.con` instead of `.com`). If the address is wrong, Sign Up again with the correct one.
4. **Ask for help**: If it still does not arrive, open <a href="live-chat.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Live Chat</a> from the bubble in the bottom-right corner. Support can verify the account and help you activate it.
