# ISP Proxies console

ISP Proxies are carrier ISP dedicated exits. Modes are **Static** and **Custom**. They fit B2B portals, enterprise systems, and sessions that must keep the same `host:port`.

In the left sidebar **Proxies** group, click **ISP Proxies**:

<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-proxy-isp.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Open the ISP Proxies console</a> · <a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/products/proxy-isp.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ISP Proxies product page</a>

For GB-billed commercial broadband rotation, use the <a href="business-proxies.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Business Proxies console</a>.

---

## Product modes

ISP Proxies are not sold as Rotating traffic packs. The console has two dedicated modes:

1. **Static**:  
   A dedicated ISP exit. The entry point stays for the plan period. You can request a new exit IP in the console.  
   - Purchase: <a href="../getting-started/static/purchase.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Purchase Static Proxies</a>
   - Inventory: <a href="../getting-started/static/view-lines.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">View purchased lines</a>
   - Change exit: <a href="../getting-started/static/refresh-ip.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Change exit IP</a>
2. **Custom**:  
   Delivered as ports. Assign country or city per port; rotate the exit on a timer or by hand.  
   - Ports: <a href="../getting-started/custom/view-ports.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">View and manage ports</a>
   - Renew: <a href="../getting-started/custom/auto-renew.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Auto-renew</a>

## Common tasks

- **Buy a line**: On **Buy**, pick the ISP geo and rental term, then pay.
- **Whitelist**: On **Users & Whitelist**, add your office or crawler public IP. Static and Custom ISP lines can then connect without a password. See <a href="../getting-started/static/authentication.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Users & Whitelist</a>.
- **Auto-renew**: Before expiry, turn on <a href="../getting-started/static/auto-renew.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Auto-renew</a> on **My Proxies**.
