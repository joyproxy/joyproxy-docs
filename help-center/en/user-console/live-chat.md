# Live Chat

Live Chat is JoyProxy’s real-time support channel. Use it when you are choosing a plan, stuck on first-time proxy setup, or need a fast check on service status.

---

## How to open it

You can start a chat in two places:

1. **Console Live Chat page**  
   Click **Support** in the left sidebar, or open <a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-support.html?tab=chat" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Live Chat</a> in the browser.
2. **Bottom-right bubble**  
   On the JoyProxy homepage or any console page, click the support bubble in the bottom-right corner to open the chat widget.

---

## When Live Chat is the better fit

- **Pre-sales**: Unsure whether fingerprint-browser isolation, multi-store e-commerce, or a crawler should use Rotating Residential or Static Dedicated;
- **Setup**: User/Pass failures in Chrome, Firefox, AdsPower, or Python/Scrapy that you want to debug live;
- **Recharge and orders**: You just paid or bought a plan and want a human to confirm it landed;
- **Incidents**: Sudden network errors — confirm whether a node is in maintenance or the whole service is degraded.

---

## What to send first

To help support diagnose in a few minutes, have ready:

1. **The account email**;
2. **The product line** (Rotating Residential, Static Dedicated, or Web Scraping API);
3. **The client** (Python requests, cURL, AdsPower, Browser Extension, and so on);
4. **A full error screenshot or terminal output** (mask passwords and Master User Token).

---

## Create ticket from chat

If the issue is large (cross-facility routing, a large billing recon, or a config change that needs engineering), staff can turn the chat into a Tickets thread with **Create ticket**. You do not have to restate the context.
