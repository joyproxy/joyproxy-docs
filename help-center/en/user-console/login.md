# Log In

The JoyProxy console is where you manage proxy inventory, API keys, and Invoices.

<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/login.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Open the Log In page</a>

---

## Sign-in methods

### 1. Email and password
Enter the email and password from Sign Up, then click **Sign In**. If you forgot the password, click **Forgot password?** under the password field and reset it by email. See <a href="forgot-password.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Forgot password</a>.

### 2. Google / GitHub
If you created the account with OAuth, or you want to skip typing a password, click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub** below the divider. After the browser authorization, you enter the console.

---

## Common issues

### Account not activated
If email sign-in reports that the account is not activated, you have not opened the confirmation link yet. On the Log In page, click **Resend activation email**, then open the message in your inbox (and spam). Details: <a href="email-activation.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Email activation</a>.

### Forgot password
Click **Forgot password?** under the form, enter the registered email, and follow the reset link in the official message. After you set a new password, Log In again.

---

## Daily security

- **Idle sign-out time**: On shared networks, coworking desks, or public devices, open **My Account** → **Security & API** and shorten <a href="idle-sign-out.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Idle sign-out time</a> (for example 1 hour or 3 hours).
- **Protect tokens**: Automation tokens can extract proxies or place paid orders. Do not paste them into public chats, Tickets, or unencrypted repos.
- **Sign out on shared devices**: When you finish on someone else’s computer, click **Logout** at the bottom of the left sidebar to clear the browser session.
