# Business Proxies and ISP Proxies

- [Business Proxies console](business-proxies.md) — Rotating Proxies (GB). [Open console](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-proxy-business.html)
- [ISP Proxies console](isp-proxies.md) — Static and Custom. [Open console](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-proxy-isp.html)
