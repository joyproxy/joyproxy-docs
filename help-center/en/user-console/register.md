# Sign Up

JoyProxy offers flexible sign-up and sign-in options. You can create an account without a credit card, then purchase proxy traffic or try Web Scraping API after you Log In.

<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/register.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Open the Sign Up page</a>

---

## Recommended: third-party OAuth

If you already have a Google or GitHub account, use OAuth on the Sign Up page. Click the matching button at the top of the form:

- **Continue with Google**
- **Continue with GitHub**

After you authorize, JoyProxy creates and links your account automatically. You do not need to remember a password or complete email activation. Later visits can use the same one-click authorization.

---

## Email and password

If you prefer a work or personal email:

1. In **Or sign up with email**, enter an address you check regularly.
2. Create a password (at least 8 characters; mix of letters and numbers is recommended).
3. Complete the Cloudflare Turnstile check when the page asks for it.
4. Click **Create Account**.

JoyProxy then emails an activation link to that address. Open the link to activate the account, then Log In. For the full activation flow, see <a href="email-activation.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Email activation</a>.

---

## First steps after Sign Up

1. **Log In to the console**: After you <a href="login.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Log In</a>, you land on <a href="overview.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Overview</a>. The top board shows your Available Balance.
2. **Pick a proxy network**: In the left sidebar **Proxies** group, choose the product that fits the job (Residential for e-commerce scraping, Mobile for social apps, or Datacenter for high-throughput crawls).
3. **Get API keys**: To manage orders or scraping from code, open **My Account** → **Security & API** for your account keys. See <a href="rotate-token.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Rotate token</a>.

For network pricing, see the <a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/pricing.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">JoyProxy pricing page</a>.
