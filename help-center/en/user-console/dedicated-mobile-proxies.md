# Dedicated 4G/5G Mobile Proxy Console

Open **Proxy Services → Dedicated 4G/5G** (`/admin-proxy-mobile-dedicated.html`) in the left sidebar to reserve and manage your Dedicated 4G/5G Mobile Proxy:

- **Reserve / Buy Tab**: Filter 17 countries and 31 dedicated 4G/5G carriers (including `5G Only`), pick a **2 / 7 / 14 / 30-day** period and quantity, and click **Reserve · 24h Activation** (*note: non-refundable once delivered*).
- **My Proxies Tab**: View live status counters (Pending Activation / Active / Expiring / Expired), edit inline notes, copy HTTP/SOCKS5/antidetect configs, switch IP on demand, copy your private switch API link, renew, or open a support ticket.
- **Overview & Billing Sync**: Purchases and renewals appear in your **Overview** 6-month spending chart, asset summary, and **Billing & Transactions**.
