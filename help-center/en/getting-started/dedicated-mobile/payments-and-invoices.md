# Payments and Invoices

Every Dedicated 4G/5G purchase, reservation, and renewal is logged automatically in your billing ledger:

1. Open **My Account → Billing & Transactions** (`/admin-transactions.html`) in the console.
2. Filter by **Dedicated Real-Device 4G/5G Proxies** or **Dedicated 4G/5G Renewal** to view order numbers, amounts, payment methods, and timestamps.
3. Download **PDF Invoices / Receipts** directly from the transaction list for accounting or reimbursement.
4. Track monthly Dedicated 4G/5G spend alongside other products in the **Overview** 6-month spending chart.
