# Payments and Invoices

Every Dedicated Mobile purchase, reservation, and renewal is logged automatically in your billing ledger:

1. Open **My Account → Billing & Transactions** (`/admin-transactions.html`) in the console.
2. Filter by **Dedicated Mobile Proxies** or **Dedicated Mobile Renewal** to view order numbers, amounts, payment methods, and timestamps.
3. Download **PDF Invoices / Receipts** directly from the transaction list for accounting or reimbursement.
4. Track monthly Dedicated Mobile spend alongside other products in the **Overview** 6-month spending chart.
