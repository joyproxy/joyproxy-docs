# Restricted Targets

To maintain carrier compliance and network integrity, Dedicated 4G/5G Mobile Proxy must not be used for:

- Illegal activity under applicable laws (fraud, unauthorized access, phishing, malware, carding);
- Bulk spam email (SMTP ports 25/465/587 are blocked), DDoS attacks, port scanning, or credential stuffing;
- Government, military, or critical infrastructure targets that prohibit proxy access;
- Intellectual property infringement or abusive bandwidth disruption.

Violations of carrier policies or our [Acceptable Use Policy](https://www.joyproxy.com/terms.html) may result in immediate suspension without refund.
