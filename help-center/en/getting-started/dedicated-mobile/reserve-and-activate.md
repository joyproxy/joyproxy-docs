# Reservation & 24-Hour Activation

## Why Reservation Mode?

Dedicated Static Mobile Proxies are not software pools — every port maps to a dedicated physical 4G/5G SIM card slot on a real mobile carrier network.

- **Instant Buy (Sold Out)**: Disabled in Phase 1 until automated instant-stock provisioning opens.
- **Reserve · 24h Activation**: Primary checkout flow. Once paid, your order enters our provisioning queue and our ops team allocates, tests, and delivers your dedicated slot within **24 hours**.

## When Does the Rental Clock Start?

Your **2 / 7 / 14 / 30-day** period starts strictly from the moment the port is **activated and delivered (`activated_at`)**. Waiting time before activation is never deducted.

Once ready, you receive an email notification and the port status switches to **Active** in your console.

## Ordering Notes & Payment Methods

- **Rental Periods**: 2 days (quick test), 7 days, 14 days, or 30 days — all with unlimited traffic.
- **Payment Methods**: JoyProxy account balance (including top-up bonus), WeChat Pay, USDT (TRC20), Credit Card, PayPal, and more.
- **Multi-Port Orders**: Ordering quantity > 1 automatically creates separate port records so you can add notes, switch IPs on demand, and renew each port independently.
- **Important**: Dedicated Mobile Proxies are a special provisioned physical-device product and are **non-refundable once delivered**. Please verify country, carrier, and duration before checkout.
