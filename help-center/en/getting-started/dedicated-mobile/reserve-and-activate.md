# Reservation & 24-Hour Activation

## Why Reservation Mode?

Every Dedicated Mobile Proxy is backed by a real physical 4G/5G SIM card and cellular modem rather than a virtual shared pool.

- **Instant Activate button**: Shown as greyed **Sold Out** during Phase 1 (will unlock when automated instant inventory is enabled).
- **Reserve Now · Ready in 24h button**: Primary checkout flow. Once paid, your reservation enters our priority provisioning queue and is activated within **24 hours**.

## When Does My Rental Clock Start?

Your **2 / 7 / 14 / 30-day** rental duration starts strictly at **actual delivery (`activated_at`)** — zero waiting time is deducted from your subscription. You will receive an email notification immediately upon activation.

## Supported Periods & Payment Methods

- **Rental durations**: 2 days (trial), 7 days, 14 days, and 30 days — all with unlimited data traffic.
- **Payment channels**: JoyProxy account balance, WeChat Pay, USDT (TRC20), Credit Card, and PayPal.
