# Purchase and Reservation

Dedicated Mobile Proxies support both **direct instant purchase** and **reservation checkout** across **2, 7, 14, and 30-day** unlimited plans.

## 1. Direct Purchase vs Reservation (When Sold Out)

- **Direct Purchase**: When instant stock is available for your chosen carrier, click **Buy Now** to activate immediately.
- **Reservation Checkout**: Because physical 4G/5G SIM slots are finite hardware resources, if instant stock displays **Sold Out**, simply click **Reserve · 24h Activation** to submit your order.
  - Your order enters our priority provisioning queue and our ops team allocates, validates, and delivers your dedicated SIM port within **24 hours**.
  - An email notification is sent immediately upon activation and the status updates to **Active**.
  - **Billing Clock**: Your 2/7/14/30-day validity starts strictly from the **actual activation timestamp (`activated_at`)** — waiting time is never deducted.

## 2. Multi-Port Orders

Ordering quantity > 1 automatically creates separate port records so each port has its own credentials, inline notes, on-demand IP switch, and independent renewal.

## 3. Payment Methods

Checkout runs through JoyProxy Payment Hub supporting **Account Balance** (including top-up bonuses), **WeChat Pay**, **Multi-chain Crypto (USDT/USDC)**, **Credit Cards**, and **PayPal**.

> **Before You Order**: Dedicated Mobile Proxies allocate exclusive physical SIM hardware and are **non-refundable once provisioned and delivered**. Please double-check country, carrier, and duration before checkout. See [Renewal and refund policy](renew-and-refund.md).
