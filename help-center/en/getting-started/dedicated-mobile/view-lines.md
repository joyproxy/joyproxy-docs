# View Purchased Ports

Open **Dedicated Mobile → My Dedicated Mobile Proxies** (`/admin-proxy-mobile-dedicated.html?tab=orders`) to monitor and manage all ports in one dashboard.

## Status Summary Bar

- **Pending Activation**: Reservation paid; ops is provisioning your dedicated SIM within 24 hours (rental clock has not started yet).
- **Active**: Port is live and ready for connections or on-demand IP switching.
- **Expiring Soon**: Less than 48 hours remaining — renew early to keep your dedicated slot.
- **Expired**: Rental term ended and forwarding has stopped.

## Inline Notes & Asset Tracking

Click the note button on any port row to tag your accounts or projects (e.g. `Claude-Main`, `TikTok-US-01`, `Amazon-Store-A`). Each row also displays the order number, country/carrier, 4G/5G tier, activation timestamp, and exact expiry time.
