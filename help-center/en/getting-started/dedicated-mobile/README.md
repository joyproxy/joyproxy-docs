# Dedicated Static Mobile Proxies (Real 4G/5G) Guide

## 1. Product Overview

**Dedicated Mobile Proxies** are powered by real physical 4G/5G SIM card slots on genuine carrier networks. Each port is **100% dedicated static mobile**, comes with **unlimited traffic (∞)**, and supports direct **HTTP/HTTPS & SOCKS5** connections.

Unlike per-GB rotating proxy pools, Dedicated Mobile Proxies give you a **fixed, stable mobile endpoint** throughout your rental period. Your IP stays steady by default, and whenever your workflow actually needs a fresh IP, you can switch it **on demand** in the console or via your private API link.

Backed by carrier **CGNAT** trust, real mobile IPs offer the highest anti-ban reputation for:

- **AI Accounts**: Long-lived stable sessions for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor, and Midjourney.
- **Social Media**: TikTok, Instagram, X, Facebook, and YouTube account management and content publishing with unlimited bandwidth.
- **E-Commerce & Payments**: Amazon, TikTok Shop, Shopify, eBay, PayPal, and Stripe store isolation.

## 2. Reservation & 24-Hour Activation

1. Sign in to the JoyProxy Console and open **Dedicated Mobile** (`/admin-proxy-mobile-dedicated.html`).
2. Select your target country, 4G/5G carrier, and rental period (**2 / 7 / 14 / 30 days**).
3. Click **Reserve · 24h Activation** and complete payment via Balance, WeChat Pay, USDT (TRC20), Card, or PayPal (*Instant Buy is currently marked Sold Out in Phase 1*).
4. Our operations team provisions and tests your dedicated physical SIM slot within **24 hours** and notifies you by email. **Your rental clock starts only when the port is actually activated.**

## 3. Connection & On-Demand IP Switch

Under **My Dedicated Mobile Proxies**, active ports display:

- **Host / HTTP Port / SOCKS5 Port / Username / Password** with one-click copy for HTTP, SOCKS5, and antidetect browser formats.
- **Inline Notes**: Label each port with your project or account name for easy management.
- **On-Demand IP Switch**: Keep a stable static mobile IP by default; click **Switch IP** or call your private API URL whenever you choose to obtain a new IP from the same carrier (takes 5–15s, 45s cooldown).
