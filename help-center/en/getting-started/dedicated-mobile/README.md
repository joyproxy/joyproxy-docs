# Dedicated 4G/5G Real-Device Mobile Proxies Guide

## 1. Overview

**Dedicated Mobile Proxies** run on exclusive physical 4G/5G SIM card slots with **unlimited data traffic (∞)** and direct HTTP/HTTPS & SOCKS5 connectivity.

Powered by mobile carriers' **CGNAT** infrastructure, real mobile IPs carry the highest trust reputation online—making them ideal for:

- **AI Anti-Ban**: Protecting Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor, and Midjourney accounts from risk blocks.
- **Social Media Matrices**: Running TikTok, Instagram, X, Facebook, and YouTube multi-account workflows without per-GB bandwidth anxiety.
- **Cross-Border E-Commerce**: Single-tenant hardware isolation for Amazon, TikTok Shop, Shopify, PayPal, and Stripe.

---

## 2. Reservation & 24-Hour Activation

1. Open **Dedicated Mobile** in your JoyProxy console (`/admin-proxy-mobile-dedicated.html`).
2. Select country, 4G/5G carrier, IP rotation mode, and rental duration (**2 / 7 / 14 / 30 days**).
3. Click **Reserve Now · Ready in 24h** and complete checkout via Balance, WeChat Pay, USDT (TRC20), Credit Card, or PayPal (*Instant Activate is temporarily marked Sold Out during Phase 1*).
4. Our team provisions your physical SIM port within **24 hours** and notifies you via email. **Your rental period starts strictly upon delivery.**

---

## 3. Proxy Setup & IP Rotation

Under **My Proxies**, active ports display:

- **Direct Host, HTTP Port, SOCKS5 Port, Username, Password**, with one-click copy presets for HTTP, SOCKS5, and AdsPower / BitBrowser.
- **One-Click Rotate IP**: Trigger a fresh carrier IP directly from the console (takes 5–15 seconds, 45s cooldown).
- **Private Rotation API URL**: Copy your JoyProxy rotation endpoint to integrate automated IP switching into anti-detect browsers or scripts.

---

## 4. Renewals & Support Refunds

- **Renewals**: Click **Renew** on any port to extend by 2, 7, 14, or 30 days.
- **Issues & Refunds**: Click **Ticket** on the order row to reach support. Verified provisioning or carrier issues are promptly replaced or refunded to your JoyProxy account balance.
