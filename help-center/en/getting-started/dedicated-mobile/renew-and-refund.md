# Renewals & Support Ticket Refunds

## 1. How to Renew

Please submit renewals at least **24 hours** before expiry (12 hours for 2-day plans):

1. Open **Dedicated Mobile → My Proxies**.
2. Click **Renew**, pick **2 / 7 / 14 / 30 days**, and complete payment.
3. Our ops team extends your upstream carrier slot and emails you once your new expiry date is updated.

## 2. Support Tickets & Balance Refunds

If you encounter any provisioning delay, verified carrier issue, or need a refund, click **Ticket** on the order row (your order number is pre-filled). Our team will either replace the port or credit a manual refund directly to your JoyProxy account balance.
