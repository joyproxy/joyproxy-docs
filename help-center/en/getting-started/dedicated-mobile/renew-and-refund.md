# Renewal, Non-Refundable Policy & Support

## 1. How to Renew

Please renew at least **24 hours before expiry** (or 12 hours for 2-day plans) so your physical SIM slot is not released by the carrier:

1. Open **Dedicated Mobile → My Dedicated Mobile Proxies**.
2. Click **Renew** on the port row, choose **2 / 7 / 14 / 30 days**, and complete payment.
3. Once ops confirms the upstream renewal, your expiry time extends automatically and an email confirmation is sent.

## 2. Non-Refundable Special Product Policy

> **Important Notice**: **Dedicated Static Mobile Proxies** allocate exclusive physical 4G/5G SIM card slots whose carrier costs are incurred immediately upon provisioning. **Once provisioned and delivered, Dedicated Mobile Proxies are strictly non-refundable** (no free trial, no account balance refund, and no refund to the original payment method). See our [Terms of Service — Refund Policy](https://www.joyproxy.com/terms.html#refund).

If you are testing a new target platform or carrier, we strongly recommend starting with a **2-day** reservation first.

## 3. Ticket Support & Provisioning Exceptions

Click **Support Ticket** on the order row (your order number is pre-filled automatically) for:

- **Connectivity Troubleshooting**: If an active port experiences connectivity or IP-switch issues, support will investigate and replace the port where verified (not refunded).
- **Unable to Provision Within 24 Hours (Balance Refund Exception)**: Only if a reservation cannot be provisioned within **24 hours** due to upstream carrier stock shortage — or if a renewal cannot be extended — support will issue a manual refund to your **JoyProxy account balance**.
