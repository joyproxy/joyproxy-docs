# Renewal and Refund Policy

## 1. How to Renew

Renew at least **24 hours before expiry** (or 12 hours for 2-day plans) to avoid carrier slot release:

1. Open **Dedicated 4G/5G → My Proxies**.
2. Click **Renew** on the port row, choose **2 / 7 / 14 / 30 days**, and complete payment.
3. Once upstream extension is confirmed, your expiry date extends automatically and an email confirmation is sent.

## 2. Non-Refundable Special Product Policy

> **Important Notice**: **Dedicated 4G/5G Mobile Proxies** allocate exclusive physical 4G/5G SIM slots whose carrier costs are incurred immediately upon activation. **Once provisioned and delivered, orders are strictly non-refundable** (no free trial, no balance refund, and no refund to the original payment method). See our [Terms of Service — Refund Policy](https://www.joyproxy.com/terms.html#refund).

We recommend starting with a **2-day** plan when testing a new platform or carrier.

## 3. Ticket Support & Provisioning Exceptions

Click **Support Ticket** on the order row (order number is pre-filled) for:
- **Connectivity Issues**: Verified line or IP-switch faults are resolved via technical troubleshooting or port replacement (non-refundable).
- **Unable to Provision Within 24h**: Only if a reservation cannot be delivered within **24 hours** due to upstream carrier stock shortage — or if a renewal cannot be extended — support will issue a manual refund to your **JoyProxy account balance**.
