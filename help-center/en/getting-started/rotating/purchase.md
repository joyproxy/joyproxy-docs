# Purchase traffic

Rotating Proxies are billed by **GB**. The full pack is available during the validity period. Larger packs have a lower unit price.

## Choose a network type

JoyProxy offers three Rotating Proxies networks. After you log in, compare and buy them on **<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-purchase.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Purchase Proxies</a>** (<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/pricing.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">see current pricing</a>):

| Network type | How to choose | Typical use cases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rotating Residential Proxies** | Real home-broadband IPs in 200+ countries | Data collection, web crawling, e-commerce price checks, account ops |
| **Rotating Mobile Proxies** | Real 4G / 5G carrier IPs, very hard to block | Mobile app testing, mobile ad verification, high-risk retries |
| **Rotating Business / ISP Proxies** | Enterprise ISP exits — fast and high-trust | B2B portal integration, long-lived API calls, business-platform workflows |

One account can hold traffic packs for several networks at the same time. Each order is metered on its own.

## Buy in the console

1. Log in and open **<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-purchase.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Purchase Proxies</a>** in the left menu.
2. Choose a network (**Residential** / **Mobile** / **Business / ISP**).
3. Open the **Rotating Proxies** tab.
4. Pick a traffic pack that matches your scale (for example 5 GB, 10 GB, 50 GB, or 100 GB).
5. Choose a payment method and complete checkout:
   - **Balance** (no extra fee)
   - **PayPal**
   - **Credit Card** (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, JCB)
   - **Apple Pay** / **Google Pay**
   - **WeChat Pay**
   - **UPI** (India)
   - **USDT (TRC20)** (TRON, 1 USDT = $1)
6. After payment succeeds, the pack is activated immediately in the console.

## After you buy

- When traffic is active, go to <a href="view-traffic.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">View purchased traffic</a> to confirm order status and remaining GB.
- Open <a href="authentication.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Users & Whitelist</a> and create a proxy Password.
- Then generate endpoints on **<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-ip-extraction-center.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Endpoint generator</a>**.
