# Payments and invoices

The JoyProxy console lists full transaction history and lets you download official PDF invoices / receipts.

---

## 1. View payment history

1. Log in and open **<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-transactions.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Invoices</a>** in the left menu.
2. In the list you can inspect each transaction:
   - **Order ID**
   - **Type**: Purchase, Recharge, Renew, and others.
   - **Amount and channel**: USD amount and method (Credit Card, PayPal, WeChat Pay, USDT, and others).
   - **Time and status**.

---

## 2. Download receipts and invoices

- **Download a PDF receipt**: on **<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-transactions.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Invoices</a>**, find a completed payment and click **PDF** on the right to download an official stamped PDF receipt.
- **Request a company invoice**: to get an invoice with a company name, contact **<a href="../../user-console/live-chat.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Live Chat</a>** with the billing name and tax ID. Support will issue it and send it to the email you specify.
