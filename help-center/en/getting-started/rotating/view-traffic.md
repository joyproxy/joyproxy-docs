# View purchased traffic

In the console you can check Rotating Proxies pack status, used traffic, and remaining GB at any time.

## Steps

1. Log in to the JoyProxy console.
2. Open **<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-my-orders.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">My Proxies</a>** in the left menu.
3. Switch to the **Rotating Proxies** section. You will see cards for every active Rotating Proxies order:
   - **Pack name and network type**: for example Rotating Residential Proxies.
   - **Traffic quota**: Used (GB) and Remaining (GB).
   - **Traffic progress bar**: share of the pack already consumed.
   - **Purchase Time and Validity**.

## How traffic is billed and how long it lasts

- **Bidirectional billing**: usage is the sum of HTTP/HTTPS upload and download bytes for the request.
- **Reuse without reordering**: as long as remaining GB is greater than zero, generated endpoints keep working. You do not buy again for each extract.
- **Auto refill**: to keep crawlers and automation from stopping when a pack runs out, turn on **<a href="auto-buy-traffic.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Auto-buy traffic</a>**.
