# Payments and invoices

Every Web Scraping API credit-pack purchase is listed in the console, and you can download an official invoice immediately.

---

## How to download

1. Log in to the JoyProxy console and open **<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-transactions.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Invoices</a>**.
2. Find the credit-pack order in the list.
3. Click **PDF** on the right of the row.
4. The console downloads an official PDF receipt.

---

## Company invoices

If you need a commercial invoice with your company name, open **<a href="../../user-console/live-chat.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Live Chat</a>** in the lower-right corner of the console and send the billing details to support.
