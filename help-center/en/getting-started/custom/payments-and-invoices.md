# View payments and download invoices

Every Custom Proxies purchase and renewal is recorded, and you can download an official electronic invoice immediately.

---

## Steps

1. Log in to the JoyProxy console and open **<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-transactions.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Invoices</a>**.
2. Filter the ledger by date and type (purchase, renewal, recharge).
3. Click **PDF** on a row.
4. The official invoice PDF is generated for you to save or print.

---

## Company-name invoices

If you need a formal invoice with company name, tax ID, and address:
- Open **<a href="../../user-console/live-chat.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Live Chat</a>** in the bottom-right corner, send the order ID and billing details, and billing support will issue it.
