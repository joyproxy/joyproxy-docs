# Auto-renew

Custom Proxies ports run on duration plans (daily / weekly / monthly / annual). To keep a port from being reclaimed and released to someone else when it expires unpaid, turn on Auto-renew for important port orders.

---

## Enable and manage

1. Log in to the JoyProxy console and open **<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-my-orders.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">My Proxies</a>**.
2. Find the Custom Proxies order card or list row.
3. Turn the **Auto-renew** switch on.
4. After it is on, about 24 hours before expiry JoyProxy deducts the next cycle from your **Account Balance** and extends the port’s validity.

---

## Notes

1. **Keep enough balance**: Your Account Balance must cover the renewal amount. If the charge fails, Auto-renew does not run and you receive an email.
2. **Expiry and reclaim**: If the order expires without a successful renewal, the port is frozen and then reclaimed after a grace period. Dedicated settings and historical IPs on that port cannot be recovered after reclaim.
3. **Turn it off anytime**: You can turn Auto-renew off at any time. The period you already paid for is unaffected; nothing is charged after expiry.
