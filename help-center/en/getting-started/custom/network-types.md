# Network types and how to choose

Custom Proxies are billed by port and duration. Three underlying networks are available (<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/pricing.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">see current pricing</a>). Understanding the difference before checkout helps you pick the right fit.

---

## Three custom dedicated networks

| Network | IP source and trust | Bandwidth and latency | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Custom Dedicated Residential Proxies** | IPs assigned by real home-broadband ISPs | Real broadband speed, highest trust, fewer captchas | Cross-border multi-store ecommerce, overseas social account warming, high-risk protected sites |
| **Custom Dedicated Business / ISP Proxies** | Carrier business ISP dedicated-line IPs | Dedicated bandwidth, fast and stable, business ASN identity | Long-lived B2B system access, cross-border enterprise automation, workloads that need a business IP |
| **Custom Dedicated Datacenter Proxies** | Enterprise datacenter / hosting ASN lines | Very low latency, 100 Mbps / 1 Gbps-class throughput, strong value | Large-scale public-data automation, high-concurrency API tests, API forwarding |

---

## Details

### 1. Custom Dedicated Residential Proxies
- **Real home-broadband trust**: Sites such as Amazon, Facebook, and Google treat the exit as a household visitor, so block rates stay low.
- **Per-port setup**: Each port can have its own country, state/province, and city, and you can turn on rotation per port.

### 2. Custom Dedicated Business / ISP Proxies
- **Business ISP lines**: Commercial office IPs from major carriers (such as AT&T, Verizon, and Comcast) — residential-like trust with datacenter-like speed and stability.
- **Built for high throughput**: A fit when you move larger payloads often and still need a high-trust IP.

### 3. Custom Dedicated Datacenter Proxies
- **Value and concurrency**: Cloud-hosted IPs with very low latency and high throughput.
- **Many ports at once**: A fit when you order 50–200 ports in one checkout for large parallel collection.
