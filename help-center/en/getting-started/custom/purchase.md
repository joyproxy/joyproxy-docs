# Purchase ports

Custom Proxies are billed by **dedicated port count** and **plan duration**. One order can include up to 200 ports. For the plan period, every port is 100% yours.

---

## Purchase flow

1. Log in to the JoyProxy console and open **<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-purchase.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Purchase Proxies</a>**.
2. Choose a network tab:
   - **Residential**
   - **Business / ISP**
   - **Datacenter**
3. In product mode, select **Custom Dedicated Residential Proxies** (or **Custom Dedicated Business Proxies** / **Custom Dedicated Datacenter Proxies** for those networks).
4. Choose a validity plan:
   - **Daily Plan**
   - **Weekly Plan**
   - **Monthly Plan**
   - **Quarterly Plan**
   - **Annual Plan** (highest discount)
5. Set **Number of ports (this order)**:
   - Enter how many ports you need (for example `10`).
   - One order supports up to `200` ports. Price formula: `order total = per-port plan price × number of ports`.
6. Configure **Pre-assign port locations** (optional):
   - If you already know some countries, allocate port counts per country at the bottom of the page.
   - Ports you do not pin at checkout stay **Assign after purchase**. After payment, you can Assign region and change geo anytime on My Proxies.
7. Choose a payment method and submit:
   - **Balance** (recommended — deducted instantly and the ports go live)
   - **PayPal**
   - **Credit Card**
   - **Apple Pay** / **Google Pay**
   - **WeChat Pay**
   - **UPI**
   - **USDT (TRC20)**
8. After payment succeeds, the ports are created and appear in your console.

---

## Volume discounts

When a single order reaches `50+` or `100+` ports, volume discounts apply automatically. The checkout preview shows the price breakdown as you type.

Next: open **<a href="view-ports.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">View and manage ports</a>** or **<a href="assign-region.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Assign region</a>** to configure the ports.
