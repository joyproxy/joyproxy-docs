# Restricted targets and compliance

To keep the network healthy and compliant, JoyProxy blocks access to certain networks and destinations.

## Mainland China restrictions

1. **No mainland China exit IPs**.
2. **You cannot connect to JoyProxy gateways from a mainland China local network**.
3. **Proxy exit IPs must not access sites in mainland China** (including names ending in `.cn` and servers hosted in mainland China).

## High-risk destinations that are blocked

- **Banks and financial institutions**: online banking, payment gateways, securities trading platforms.
- **Government and public agencies**: government portals and public-sector SSO.
- **Mail and messaging**: bulk webmail logins or sending spam.
