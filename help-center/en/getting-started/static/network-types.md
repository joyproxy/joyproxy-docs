# Network types and how to choose

Static Proxies are billed by line and duration. Three networks are available (<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/pricing.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">see current pricing</a>):

---

## Three static dedicated networks

| Network | IP source and trust | Strengths | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Static Residential Proxies** | Static IPs assigned by real home-broadband ISPs | **Very high trust**. Looks like a household visitor, not a hosting ASN. | Ecommerce seller accounts, long-term overseas social accounts, high-trust risk-control workflows. |
| **Static Business / ISP Proxies** | Carrier business ISP dedicated lines | **High trust + dedicated bandwidth**. Faster and more stable than typical residential, with a business ISP identity. | Long-lived B2B portal access, cross-border enterprise logins, high-throughput stable transfers. |
| **Static Datacenter Proxies** | Enterprise datacenter ASN lines | **Very low latency, high throughput, strong value**. | Large-scale public data monitoring, API forwarding, performance tests. |

---

## How to choose

1. **Ecommerce and long-term social** ➔ **Static Residential Proxies** (stable geography and household trust).
2. **Enterprise dedicated links and B2B data exchange** ➔ **Static Business / ISP Proxies** (speed plus trust).
3. **High concurrency, high throughput, budget-sensitive** ➔ **Static Datacenter Proxies**.
