# View payments and download invoices

1. Open **<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-transactions.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Invoices</a>** in the console.
2. Review the ledger (purchases, renewals, recharges).
3. Click **PDF** on a row to download the official invoice PDF.
4. For a company-name invoice, contact **<a href="../../user-console/live-chat.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Live Chat</a>**.
