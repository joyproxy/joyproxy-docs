# Purchase dedicated lines

Static Proxies are billed by IP line count and plan duration. After checkout, each line is 100% yours for the plan period.

## Steps

1. Log in to the JoyProxy console and open **<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-purchase.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Purchase Proxies</a>** in the left menu.
2. Choose a network:
   - **Residential**
   - **Business / ISP**
   - **Datacenter**
3. Switch to the **Static Dedicated Residential Proxies** tab (or **Static Dedicated Business Proxies** / **Static Dedicated Datacenter Proxies** for those networks).
4. Choose a validity plan:
   - **Daily Plan**
   - **Weekly Plan**
   - **Monthly Plan**
   - **Quarterly Plan**
   - **Annual Plan** (highest discount)
5. Under **Choose IP locations**:
   - Select the target country/region (for example United States or Japan).
   - Set how many IPs you need in that region.
6. Choose a payment method and complete checkout:
   - **Balance**
   - **PayPal**
   - **Credit Card**
   - **Apple Pay** / **Google Pay**
   - **WeChat Pay**
   - **UPI**
   - **USDT (TRC20)**
7. After payment succeeds, the lines are assigned and go live immediately.

Next: open **<a href="view-lines.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">View purchased lines</a>** to confirm the assigned endpoints.
