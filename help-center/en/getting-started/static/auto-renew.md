# Auto-renew

To keep a static dedicated line from being reclaimed when it expires unpaid, turn on Auto-renew for important orders.

## Enable and manage

1. Open **<a href="https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-my-orders.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">My Proxies</a>**.
2. Find the Static Proxies order card.
3. Turn on the **Auto-renew** switch.
4. After it is on, JoyProxy charges your **Account Balance** before expiry and extends the matching plan duration.

## Notes

- Keep enough Account Balance. If the balance is too low at renewal time, Auto-renew fails and an unpaid expired line is reclaimed.
- You can turn Auto-renew off at any time. Turning it off does not affect the period you already paid for.
