# Live chat

Reach JoyProxy support in real time for purchase advice, integration questions, and account issues.

## From the console

1. Sign in and open [Support](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-support.html).
2. Switch to the **Live Chat** tab.
3. Describe your goal (product type, target region, error messages).
4. Stay on the page until an agent joins—queue times vary by hour.

## From the website

Use the **chat bubble** on [joyproxy.com](https://www.joyproxy.com/) marketing pages or the [AI Assistant product page](https://www.joyproxy.com/products/ai-customer-service.html) when you want instant automated answers first.

## Get faster help

Include:

- Account email (never your password)
- Order ID or approximate purchase time
- Endpoint type (rotating gateway vs static host)
- Redacted screenshot of error text

For billing disputes attach transaction IDs from [Transactions](https://www.joyproxy.com/admin-transactions.html).

## Alternatives

- Detailed technical paper trail: [Ticket system](tickets.md)
- Self-serve: this documentation and [FAQ](../faq/README.md)
