## How they work together

- Deploy **joyproxy-server** on your VPS, or point clients at JoyProxy cloud generated endpoints
- Configure server auth (open, whitelist, password, or API)
- Connect via the **Browser Extension**, **Proxy Client** app, or any HTTP/SOCKS5 client
